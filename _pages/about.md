[Austin Buchanan](https://ceat.okstate.edu/iem/people/buchanan-faculty-profile.html) is an Associate Professor and Graduate Program Director of [Industrial Engineering & Management](https://ceat.okstate.edu/iem/) at [Oklahoma State University](https://go.okstate.edu/), and holder of the Dean's Professorship. His research has been funded by the National Science Foundation (including an NSF CAREER award) and the Air Force Office of Scientific Research. His papers have appeared in journals such as _Operations Research_, _Mathematical Programming_, _Mathematical Programming Computation_, _INFORMS Journal on Computing_, _INFORMS Journal on Optimization_, _Networks_, and _Election Law Journal_, among others.
